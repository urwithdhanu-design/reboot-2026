# Enterprise insurance guidance

## Best policies by category

### Home insurance
Home Protect Plus is our flagship buildings and contents cover for UK homeowners. It includes optional accidental damage, legal expenses, and flexible excess. Best for customers who want straightforward cover with digital policy management and on-chain policy certificates after minting.

### Motor insurance
Comprehensive and third-party motor products cover private vehicles with optional breakdown and legal cover. Competitive for low-mileage drivers and multi-car households when bundled with home cover.

### Pet insurance
Lifetime pet cover supports ongoing conditions with annual benefit resets. Accident-only tiers suit younger pets with lower premiums.

### Travel insurance
Single-trip and annual multi-trip travel cover includes medical emergencies, cancellation, and baggage. Winter sports and cruise extensions are available at quote stage.

### Health and life
Vitality-linked health plans reward active lifestyles. Life cover provides lump-sum protection with optional critical illness riders.

## Business rules — home policy (summary)

- Cover starts after successful premium payment and policy minting where applicable.
- Claims must relate to an insured peril listed in the policy wording.
- Excess applies per claim unless waived under a named promotion.
- Pre-existing structural issues must be disclosed at quote; non-disclosure may void cover.
- Cancellations within cooling-off period receive pro-rata refund subject to no claims.

## Business rules — motor policy (summary)

- Named drivers only unless comprehensive policy includes any-driver extension.
- Mileage bands affect premium; exceeding declared mileage may affect claims.
- Modifications must be declared before inception.
- Windscreen and glass claims may have separate excess.

## Business rules — claims process

- Report claims promptly via the app or phone with policy number and incident date.
- Evidence (photos, receipts, police reports) speeds assessment.
- Settled claims appear as settled, paid, or paid_out in customer records.
- Parametric triggers may auto-settle when verified against external data feeds.

## How to get personal policy data

Logged-in customers can ask Stallion for **my policy details** or **claims settled on my policy**. Guest users should register or log in for account-specific answers.
